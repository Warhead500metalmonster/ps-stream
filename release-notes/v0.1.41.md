## What's changed

- **Far fewer sound dropouts during play.** In real sessions, sound often arrived with gaps of 150 to 300 ms even with no packet loss at all, several times a minute. The audio buffer could never grow past 100 ms, so every one of those gaps was a dropout, and the **Max delay** setting couldn't change that. Now:
  - After a dropout, the buffer grows straight to the length of the gap that caused it, up to 300 ms, instead of 10 ms at a time.
  - **Max delay** really sets the limit. The buffer can grow to two thirds of it. The default is now 400 ms. If yours was still at the old default of 150 ms, it moves to 400 ms. A value you picked yourself stays.
  - On Wi-Fi that delivers sound evenly, the buffer stays as small as before, and it shrinks again after a quiet minute.
  - In a simulation built from those sessions, dropouts went from about 12 to 4 a minute in audio only and from 7 to 2 with video, and silence from dropouts went from 1.7 s to 0.2 s a minute. On bad Wi-Fi, the sound can trail by up to about 0.3 s. If you'd rather have less delay than fewer dropouts, lower **Max delay** under **Advanced settings**.
- **Updates install by themselves again after a bad one.** After v0.1.39 failed three times, the app stopped installing every version after it by itself, so v0.1.40 had to be installed by hand. The limit now applies only to the version that failed.

Connecting wasn't changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off. On older versions, reopen the app and press **Update** at the bottom.

Or download `PS-Stream-v0.1.41-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes about 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Some people report that a gamepad on the PC doesn't control the PS5. If that happens to you, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
