# PS Stream 1.3.11

## Fixed: broken-up sound during games

When the sound fell behind, the app caught up by leaving out a 10 ms piece of sound once a second, even when that piece wasn't quiet. Games like GTA 6 are rarely quiet, so the sound broke up all the time. Now only quiet pieces are left out, like in chiaki. If the delay grows too long, the app still cuts it down after a burst from the PS5.

In a test with GTA-like Wi-Fi gaps, the app left out 291 pieces of sound in 30 seconds before. Now it leaves out none.

## Fixed: long silences after a short gap

After the sound ran out, the app stayed quiet until it had a full buffer again, which could take up to half a second. Now it plays again once 50 ms of sound has arrived, like chiaki. The longest silence in the lost-packet test went from 690 ms to 410 ms, and the sound runs about 50 ms behind instead of 240 ms.

Nothing about connecting or the picture changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.11-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If version 1.3.8 or older stopped updating and `PS Stream.exe` is missing from the top of its folder, download the zip once and extract it over that folder. While 1.3.8 or older installs this update, wait for it to reopen by itself instead of opening it again.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
