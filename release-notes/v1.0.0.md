## PS Stream 1.0

The first full release, just in time for GTA 6. PS Stream is a PS5 Remote Play client for Windows, made for people on weak Wi-Fi who can't use a cable or 5 GHz. Play on your TV and hear the game on your PC, or stream it to Discord.

This version has the same code as v0.1.44 and passed every test again. The version number says it's ready.

### What's in it
- **Separate audio and video switches.** Audio only asks the PS5 for the smallest stream and doesn't decode video.
- **Made for bad Wi-Fi:**
  - The audio buffer grows to the gaps your Wi-Fi actually has, up to 0.5 s in audio only and 0.3 s with video, and shrinks again when the Wi-Fi calms down.
  - After a stall, the sound catches up without a second silence.
  - Lost packets are resent, and acks the PS5 missed are sent again, so a weak link doesn't end the stream early.
  - The app asks for a fresh picture when one is lost, and starts a decoder that froze over again.
  - It reconnects by itself, finds the PS5 again if its address changes, and tells you when the router changes its Wi-Fi channel.
- **Video window:** `F11` or maximize for full screen. It keeps the picture's shape when resized, and has no black bars.
- **Connects by itself and wakes the PS5.** It runs in the tray, starts with Windows if you want, and has global hotkeys.
- **Keeps itself up to date** from GitHub, checking every download against GitHub's checksum. A broken update rolls back, and only that version is skipped.
- **Turkish and English.**

### Built and checked on GitHub
Every release is built from the code in this repository. Before it goes out, the build checks that every library loads, that sound, video and gamepad start, and that the files carry their version details. The download includes `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.0.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**. See [Windows Defender says it's a trojan](https://github.com/Warhead500metalmonster/ps-stream#windows-defender-says-its-a-trojan).

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes about 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- A gamepad on the PC hasn't been confirmed to control the PS5 yet. If it doesn't for you, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
- When the Wi-Fi brings nothing at all for more than half a second, the sound stops for that long. No app can fill that gap.
- The app isn't signed, so Windows Defender may flag a new version now and then.
