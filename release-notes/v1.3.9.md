# PS Stream 1.3.9

## Fixed: updates stopped working

- **Opening the app during an update broke the installation.** The copy opened then deleted the files kept for going back, and the updater undid the update without them: the launcher `PS Stream.exe` and two license files were gone. Now a copy opened during an update leaves, and the update opens the app when it's done.
- **A missing launcher no longer stops updates.** The app used to stop installing updates, without saying why, when that file was missing. Now it updates anyway and puts the file back.
- **Going back no longer deletes a file it can't replace.**
- The log says when an update can't install itself.

Tested in a separate folder with a local update server: 5 of 8 timings of opening the app during an update lost the files before, and none do now. An install without the launcher updates, and a broken update still goes back completely.

Nothing about connecting, the picture or the sound changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.9-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If version 1.3.8 or older stopped updating and `PS Stream.exe` is missing from the top of its folder, download the zip once and extract it over that folder. While 1.3.8 or older installs this update, wait for it to reopen by itself instead of opening it again.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
