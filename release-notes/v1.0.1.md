## What's changed

- **Only the picture itself is drawn.** A friend watching a stream on Discord, and the video window itself, showed a thin green band with garbage along the bottom of the picture in a 720p H.264 stream. The graphics card's decoder keeps each frame in a texture taller than the picture, and the extra rows were meant to stay out through a setting some graphics drivers may not follow. Now the visible picture is copied into a texture of exactly its size first, and only that is drawn, so the extra rows can't be shown on any driver. If a graphics card can't do that, the app draws the way it did before. Tested with 1080p video, whose texture has 8 extra rows: the picture's own bottom edge shows to the last row.
- **The log now records what the video decoder says.** The band couldn't be reproduced here, so the log now shows the stream's coded size and the decoder's own warnings (up to 40 each time video starts), such as a part of a frame it couldn't decode. If the band is still there in this version, the log will say why.

Connecting and sound weren't changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.0.1-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes about 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- A gamepad on the PC hasn't been confirmed to control the PS5 yet. If it doesn't for you, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
- If a band still shows along the bottom of the picture, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
