## What's changed

- **A gamepad on the PC now works in PS5 games.** The app told the PS5 that a PC pad was a DUALSHOCK 4. PS5 games don't accept a DUALSHOCK 4, so a pad could move around the home screen and PS4 games but did nothing in a PS5 game. The app now says it is a DualSense, as chiaki-ng does. The log line "Session started" ends in "controller announced as a DualSense" when this is on.
- **Vibration reaches the pad.** A DualSense gets its vibration as a sound wave rather than as rumble commands, so with the change above an Xbox pad would have stopped rumbling. The app turns that wave into rumble on each side. The log says "PS5 vibration reached the gamepad" the first time in a session.
- **A quick press of Start (Options) or Back (Share) is no longer lost.** These two wait 0.12 s to see whether they are the start of a button pair (Back + Start is the PS button, Back + Y is the touchpad). A quicker press was dropped, so the pause menu only opened when Start was held. Now a quick press is sent as soon as the button is let go.
- **A button pair released one button at a time stays one press**, so the PS button and the touchpad don't flicker.
- **Tested** without a PS5: a simulated pad was found and named, and a press-by-press test of the pairs passed: a 60 ms Start tap gives Options, a held Start gives Options after 0.12 s, Back + Start gives PS for as long as either is held, and Back + Y gives the touchpad. The PS5 side hasn't been seen yet.

Sound, video and connecting weren't changed.

## How to use a gamepad

1. Plug the pad into the PC, or turn it on over Bluetooth.
2. If the gamepad setting is off, the status bar offers **Turn gamepad on**. Press it. Or turn on **PC gamepad** on the Settings page.
3. The app reconnects once, which takes 6 to 8 seconds. Then the pad plays the PS5.

Back + Start is the PS button and Back + Y is the touchpad.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
