## What's changed

- **A new look.** The app has a new icon: a Wi-Fi sign whose dot is a play button, since PS Stream is built for weak Wi-Fi. It shows in the app, the taskbar, the tray and the GitHub page, which also has a new banner.
- **The app tells you which side of the Wi-Fi is the problem.** A PS5 stream crosses Wi-Fi twice: from the PS5 to the modem, and from the modem to your PC. Discord on the same PC can be fine while the stream falls apart, because Discord only uses the PC's side.
  - While connected, the app pings the modem once a second. That's 32 bytes, nothing next to the stream.
  - When the picture stutters and the PC's side is fine, the warning says the trouble is between the PS5 and the modem.
  - After 2 minutes of a bad connection, it says that turning the modem off and on usually fixes it, with a tray note when the window is hidden. A lower quality didn't help in that case; a modem restart did.
  - The log shows the modem's answers every 30 seconds ("modem ping lost N of M, slowest X ms").
- Everything from v1.1.1 is included: the gamepad offer, the video window that fits the screen, the clearer weak-connection warning and the faster reconnect after a setting change.
- **Tested** without a PS5: the modem answered 12 of 12 pings in 5 ms or less, and the weak-connection test showed the "PC's Wi-Fi is fine" text and the 2-minute modem advice.

Sound, the video stream and the connection itself weren't changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.2.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- A gamepad on the PC hasn't been confirmed to control the PS5 yet. If it doesn't work for you, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
- Windows may keep showing the old icon on an existing shortcut until it refreshes its icon cache.
