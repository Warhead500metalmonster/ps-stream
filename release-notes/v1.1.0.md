## What's changed

- **Less data over the Wi-Fi for the same picture.** Where your graphics card decodes H.265 itself, the video presets now use it. H.265 gives about the same picture as H.264 in roughly 60% of the data, and on weak Wi-Fi less data means less of it gets lost:

  | Preset | Before (H.264) | Now (H.265) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Balanced, 720p | 6 Mbps | 3.5 Mbps |
  | High, 1080p 60 | 20 Mbps | 12 Mbps |

  - Lowest, Low and Highest stay as they were.
  - If you were on one of these presets, you move to the new one by yourself. Custom settings aren't touched.
  - On a PC whose graphics card can't decode H.265, the presets stay H.264, so nothing gets slower.
  - The app checks this at every start, so moving to another PC or graphics card is handled.
- **Tested** with 720p H.265 through the graphics card: the picture comes out whole to the last row, from a decoder texture with 48 extra rows.

Sound and connecting weren't changed. PS Stream works over your home network, so it doesn't use your internet allowance. These changes are about how much has to cross the Wi-Fi.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.1.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes about 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- A gamepad on the PC hasn't been confirmed to control the PS5 yet. If it doesn't for you, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
- If a band shows along the bottom of the picture, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
