# PS Stream 1.3.6

## What's new

- Shorter freezes when Wi-Fi breaks a frame beyond repair. The picture used to stop until the PS5 sent a whole new picture, which in a GTA 6 session took over a second most of the 42 times and once 10 seconds. The frames after it are now decoded anyway. The PS5 usually builds the next ones on an undamaged frame within a tenth of a second, and frames built on the broken one are held back for up to a second, as in 1.3.5.

Nothing about connecting or the sound changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.6-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
