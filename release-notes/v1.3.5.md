# PS Stream 1.3.5

## What's new

- No more smeared, oil-painting pictures after a lost frame. When Wi-Fi loses a whole frame, the frames after it are built on a stand-in and smear across anything that moves until the PS5 sends a whole picture again. The app now keeps the last whole picture on screen during that time instead, for at most 1 second, and goes on as soon as an undamaged frame arrives. If no whole picture comes within that second, it goes on with the damaged ones rather than standing still.
- The log says how long each damaged stretch lasted and what ended it.

Nothing about connecting or the sound changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.5-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
