# PS Stream 1.3.12

## Fixed: Windows Defender removed the app during an update

To update, the app renamed every one of its files, put the new ones in place, and then deleted the old ones. Ransomware does the same thing to files, so on 2026-11-24 Windows Defender saw the 1.3.10 update as ransomware (`Behavior:Win32/GenRansom`). It removed `PS Stream.exe` and the taskbar shortcut. After that, opening the app said the `dist` folder couldn't be found.

Now the app replaces only the files that changed, which is usually 2 or 3 instead of about 100. In a test install, an update renamed 3 files instead of 101, and a broken download still put back the old version.

If your app was removed, download the zip below and extract it over the old folder. Your settings stay. Then pin `PS Stream.exe` to the taskbar again.

Nothing about connecting, the picture or the sound changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.12-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If version 1.3.8 or older stopped updating and `PS Stream.exe` is missing from the top of its folder, download the zip once and extract it over that folder. While 1.3.8 or older installs this update, wait for it to reopen by itself instead of opening it again.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
