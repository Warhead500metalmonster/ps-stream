## What's changed

- **Balanced is sharp again where the PC decodes H.265.** Since 1.2.0, Balanced asked the PS5 for at most 3.5 Mbps with H.265. The PS5 stays well under the limit you set, so it sent at most about 2 Mbps, and a detailed game like GTA 6 looked blurry, more so when stretched to a 1080p screen or passed through Discord. Balanced now allows up to 6 Mbps, as it did with H.264, and H.265 spends that data on a cleaner picture.
- **Settings left on the old Balanced move to the new one by themselves** the first time the app starts. The log says "Quality preset 2 moves to H.265 at 6000 kbps". Custom settings aren't touched.
- **Tested** without a PS5: a settings file with the old Balanced moved to 6 Mbps, and a fresh install started at 6 Mbps.

Video decoding still runs on the graphics card. Sound, connecting and the gamepad weren't changed.

If the picture stutters on your Wi-Fi with the new Balanced, pick **Custom** in Settings and set the bitrate to 4 Mbps.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
