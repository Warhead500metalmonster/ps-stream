# PS Stream 1.3.8

Tested with made-up packets and video clips, not yet on a real PS5.

## Sound, in audio only and with video

- **Backup sound is used now.** Audio packets can repeat the pieces before their own, in case one gets lost. The receiver (from chiaki-ng) made up a missing piece before it looked at that copy, then threw the copy away. In the test with one packet in ten lost, 600 of 600 recoverable pieces were thrown away. Now none are.
- **No pile of made-up sound after an outage.** A 2.4 second outage added about 2 seconds of made-up sound at once, and real sound was then skipped to work it off. It's at most 0.1 seconds now.
- **No false 100% packet loss.** The loss count broke when packet numbers wrapped around, every 11 minutes, and when a packet arrived twice.

## Picture

- **360p no longer stops after each lost frame.** The two 360p presets use H.265 where the graphics card decodes it, as the others do. chiaki-ng can only recover from a lost reference frame in H.265: in H.264 the picture stopped until a whole new one arrived. In the test with 5% of frames lost, decoded frames went from 40% to 95%. If the PS5 refuses 360p H.265, the app goes back to H.264.
- **No flood of reports.** After one lost frame in H.264, the app sent the PS5 up to 30 reports a second. It's about 4 now.
- **Lost messages to the PS5 are resent again.** The buffer for them overflowed at 64, after which requests for a fresh picture were no longer resent. It holds 1024 now, as in chiaki-ng 1.10.1. A memory fix from that release is in too.

## Corrections to earlier notes

- 1.3.6 said it would shorten freezes. In H.264 it made the flood of reports worse, which this release fixes.
- 1.3.7 said the PS5 left frames out on purpose and that this was fixed. Real sessions showed that this wasn't so: its check never matched once in 7650 frame gaps, so that release changed nothing.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.8-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
