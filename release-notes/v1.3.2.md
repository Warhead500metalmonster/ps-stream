## What's changed

- **Fewer sound dropouts on weak Wi-Fi with video on.** Weak Wi-Fi holds sound back for 300 to 400 ms several times a minute and then delivers it all at once. The sound buffer could only grow to 266 ms, so the sound stopped at each such gap and then threw away part of the burst that followed. In one 50-minute GTA 6 session that happened 165 times. With **Max delay** left at its default, the buffer can now grow to 400 ms, and only on Wi-Fi that has such gaps. It shrinks again after quiet minutes.
- **The cost:** on such Wi-Fi the sound trails the picture by about 0.1 s more than before. If that bothers you, set **Max delay** in Settings to anything other than the default and the old limits apply.
- **Tested** without a PS5: 45 seconds of sound were fed with 250 to 400 ms gaps, the way the PS5's sound arrived in the GTA 6 sessions. Before: 11 dropouts and 95 pieces of sound thrown away. After: 3 to 5 dropouts, all in the first 10 seconds while the buffer grows, and 0 to 2 pieces thrown away.

Video, connecting and the gamepad weren't changed. Balanced stays at 6 Mbps.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
