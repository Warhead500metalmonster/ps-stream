## What's changed

- **A plugged-in gamepad is no longer ignored in silence.** The gamepad setting is off by default, and with it off the app didn't listen for pads at all, so a pad that was plugged in did nothing and nothing said why. Now the app notices the pad and the status bar offers **Turn gamepad on** for a minute. One press turns the setting on and reconnects with a controller on the PS5.
- **The video window always fits on the screen.** A window as wide as the screen was taller than the screen once its title bar was added, so the bottom of the picture sat under the taskbar. The window now opens no bigger than the screen has room for, and dragging it bigger stops there. F11 or the maximize button still fills the whole screen.
- **A clearer warning when the Wi-Fi can't carry the chosen quality.**
  - The warning now says so and points at the **Lower quality** button beside it, instead of suggesting 5 GHz to someone who has none. The 5 GHz hint still shows when a 5 GHz network of yours is in range.
  - Each new connection can warn again. Before, a warning in the last 5 minutes kept a new, heavier preset from warning.
  - With the main window in the tray, as it is when the video is full screen, a tray note says to open PS Stream to lower the quality.
- **Back up to a second sooner after changing a setting.** The PS5 keeps Remote Play "in use" for 6 to 8 seconds after the previous connection. The app now asks again every second instead of every two.
- **Tested** without a PS5: an oversized window (1920x1080 inside) opens at 1765x993 on a 1920x1080 screen and plays H.265 whole; the weak-connection test shows the new text and the button steps the quality down.

Sound, the video stream and the connection itself weren't changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.1.1-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- A gamepad on the PC hasn't been confirmed to control the PS5 yet, and the new offer wasn't tested with a real pad. If it doesn't work for you, note the time and share your `log.txt`.
- On 1080p 60 at 50 Mbps (Highest), weak 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi loses frames in busy scenes. High gives nearly the same picture at 12 Mbps.
