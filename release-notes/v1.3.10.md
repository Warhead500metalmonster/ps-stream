# PS Stream 1.3.10

## Fixed: the app closed by itself during some scenes

The PS5 blanks some scenes and menus that it won't stream, and tells the app so. The app had nothing set up for that message, so it crashed. This happened in GTA 6 on 2026-11-24. Now the status line says the PS5 isn't streaming that scene, and the picture comes back after it.

Nothing about connecting, the picture or the sound changed.

## How to update

On v0.1.22 or later, you don't need to do anything. The app installs this version by itself, either right after it opens or once audio and video are both off.

Or download `PS-Stream-v1.3.10-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `PS Stream.exe`. Keep the folder together. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your pairing and settings carry over.

If version 1.3.8 or older stopped updating and `PS Stream.exe` is missing from the top of its folder, download the zip once and extract it over that folder. While 1.3.8 or older installs this update, wait for it to reopen by itself instead of opening it again.

If Windows Defender removes `PS Stream.exe`, open **Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history**, open the PS Stream entry and choose **Restore**.

## Known issues

- With the same PSN account you play on, the DualSense stops working on the PS5 while the app is connected with a gamepad. Pair the app with a second PSN account to keep using it.
- Turning video on or off during a session reconnects. The PS5 takes 6 to 8 seconds to accept the new connection.
- Motion controls and the adaptive triggers of a real DualSense aren't sent from a PC pad.
